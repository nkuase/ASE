# The Goal of a Project

What Clients Actually Buy?

<!--

-->

---

We do the business of "Software Engineering" in the form of "Projects" to solve clients' problems; However, we sometimes forget about the real goal of project.

The Real goal of project is **not making code**.

Then, **what is the real goal of a project**?

---

## Who Are the "Clients"?

- **High-Level Leaders:** CEO, CTO  
- **Managers & Tech Leads:** your daily audience  
- **Product Owners & Sales:** shape and sell the software product  
- **Actual Paying Customers:** the ultimate decision-makers  

---

### Don't forget

Clients don’t buy *code*.  
They buy **pain relief**, **trust**, and **outcomes**.

---

## You Are a Professional Painkiller

Think beyond “developer.”  
Your real job is to deliver **UP ReST**:

- **U**nderstanding  
- **P**eace of mind  
- Risk **Re**duction  
- **S**tatus improvement  
- **T**ime recovery  

---

## 1. Understanding (U)

Clients buy your ability to **translate their real problem**.

- Understand the true goal, not just the request  
- Explore the story: how it started, why it matters, who’s affected  
- Avoid jumping to solutions before seeing the full picture  

---

## 2. Peace of Mind (P)

Clients want someone **reliable**, not just skilled.

- They want to hand off the problem without drama  
- Reliability often matters **more** than perfect code  
- Consistency builds long-term trust  

---

## 3. Risk Reduction (Re)

Clients fear failure—especially reputational risk.

- They worry about looking bad if the project collapses  
- Being the “safe choice” is more valuable than being the cheapest  
- Quick prototypes reduce uncertainty and fear  

---

## 4. Status Enhancement (S)

People buy with emotions.

- Clients wonder: “Will this make me look smart?”  
- Internal politics matter  
- Help them win internally, and you win too  

---

## 5. Time Recovery (T)

Give clients their time and energy back.

- Reduce their mental load  
- Remove the need for constant supervision  
- Let them focus on what matters most  

---

## Worst Case Scenario

- Keep saying “Everything will be OK” but no real progress  
- Not sharing updates  
- Giving inaccurate or “white lie” reports  
- Blaming others  

---

## Best Case Scenario

- Make things OK through consistent action  
- Small, steady daily progress  
- Honest updates (good or bad)  
- Learn from mistakes and prevent repeats  

---

## Software Engineers

Software Engineers are professional problem solvers who can give UpREST to clients.

- They solve problems by managing complexity.
- They solve problems using tools and rules.
- They solve problems in a team.
- They gain trust by proving them great problem solvers.

---

## Clients or Stakeholders?

In an Agile project:

- "Stakeholders" is the correct general term
- "Stakeholders" include clients, but also more people

Stakeholders = Everyone who cares about or is affected by the project
